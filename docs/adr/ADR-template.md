## Context

What forces are in play? What does the PRD currently specify, and what changed? State the
problem plainly enough that someone reading in six months understands why a decision was
needed at all — constraints, costs, deadlines, and any evidence gathered.

## Decision

The decision, stated in the active voice: "We will …". Be specific enough to be testable.
Name the alternatives that were rejected and why, in one line each.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what door this closes. Include:

- **Positive:** what this buys us.
- **Negative / cost:** what we give up, what new work it creates.
- **Follow-ups:** downstream changes required (PRD edits, new tests, new artifacts,
  dependencies on other phases).

*Every deviation from a PRD default requires an ADR committed in the same change (PRD §
"How to use this document"). Never silently change scope, targets, or stack.*
